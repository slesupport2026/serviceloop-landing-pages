Card 13 of 13 · The small stuff

Now stop counting wire nuts.

Every job eats the same handful of small materials. Nuts, screws, staples, straps, plates, a device, a few feet of wire. Chase every one of them and estimating turns into a scavenger hunt — for about eleven dollars of parts.

So we stop chasing. You set one allowance per billable hour and it covers all of it, every job, forever. This is a cost, not profit — it goes on top of your profitable rate at cost, and you never price a screw again.

Fifty dollars an hour is the floor, not a target. It has been the SLE working number for years and material has not got cheaper since.
