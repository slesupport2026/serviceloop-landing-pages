The Opportunity Offer

Answer the questions. The letter writes itself.
Start here

Build an offer a great candidate can't ignore.

One letter that sells the job, sets the standard, and protects your shop. Four steps. Most owners are done in under 30 minutes.

  1. 1

    Let your AI write the first draft Optional · saves ~15 min

    Copy the prompt below and paste it into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or whichever AI you use. It asks for your website and the role you're hiring, reads your site, and hands back a draft with your company details, mission, culture, and brand already filled in.

    Use an AI that can browse the web so it can read your site. If yours can't, it will ask you to paste your About and Careers page text instead.

  2. 2

    Bring the draft back

    Your AI replies with a block of text that starts with {. Copy all of it, paste it below, and load it. If it gave you a file instead, use Open draft at the top of the page.

    Loading replaces what's in the builder right now. Save a draft first if you want to keep it.

  3. 3

    Fill the gaps and make it yours

    • The numbers are yours. Pay, commission, sales targets, and dates are left blank on purpose. Your AI doesn't know your bank balance. You do.
    • Gold tags in the preview mark anything still missing. When they're gone, you're done.
    • Not sure what to pay at each stage? The pay calculator in section 06 shows their pay, your true cost, and whether your pricing supports it. One click drops the numbers into your offer.
    • Stuck on a question? Hit Not sure? Use a starting point and edit from there.
    • Section 09, Expectations and terms, is worth an hour with your employment attorney before you send it.
  4. 4

    Save it, send it, get it signed

    Save draftSaves every answer to a file on your computer (.json). It's your work in progress, not the letter. Close the page, come back tomorrow or on another computer, and pick up where you left off.
    Open draftLoads a draft file you saved earlier back into the builder so you can keep editing, or reuse last hire's offer as the starting point for the next one. You can also drag the file onto the page.
    Download letterSaves the finished letter as a web page (.html). Open it in any browser to read it, email it, or print it.
    Print / PDFOpens your browser's print window. Choose Save as PDF for a PDF to send, or print it on paper for signatures.

    This page also remembers your answers on this browser automatically. A saved draft file is what lets you switch computers or keep one draft per candidate.

    When you save, download, or print, a copy of the offer is stored securely with Service Loop Electrical so our team can review it with you and support the hire.
