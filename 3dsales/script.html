Sales Script Levels

Joe's process (Rev1, 11/10/26), word for word, on one page for role-play. Pick a level: Foundations, Level 1 (Opportunity or Demand call) or Level 2. The stage and step numbers match the 3D map. Homeowner lines are samples to practise against. The last section drills that level's stalls (yellow triangle) and objections (red circle).

Level
Foundations8 stages · 26 electrician lines

Start here. The same-day path in 8 stages, cut to the 26 lines that matter most, in Joe's own words.

Stalls & objections: email it over, talk to my wife, too busy; why is it so much, break down the price, over budget. Before the price: only one spouse home.

Level 1 · Opportunity call8 stages · 43 electrician lines

Joe's full same-day script for opportunity calls (upgrades and projects). Stage 5 runs the opportunity lane: build the relationship and learn what matters before offering any solution.

Stalls & objections: reviews Foundations, then unlocks think about it, other quotes, send me the prices; didn't do anything, way too much, selling me something I don't need. Before the price: email it over, how's the panel, multiple estimates.

Level 1 · Demand call8 stages · 45 electrician lines

Same as Level 1 Opportunity, but Stage 5 runs the demand lane for fix-it calls: who's involved, how the problem has affected them, and finish the diagnosis before presenting solutions.

Stalls & objections: the same as Level 1 Opportunity.

Level 2 · Full process10 stages · 58 electrician lines

Everything Joe teaches: both Stage 5 lanes, plus Stages 7–8 for the return visit (the call on route and the pulse check at the table).

Stalls & objections: reviews Foundations and Level 1, then unlocks the picture of the quote, don't call me, the spouse won't join; all about price, I could do it myself, other estimates (trust). Before the price: don't upsell me, don't make it too expensive.
