### Week 1: Profit First

**Purpose:** Build their Simple Service Rate and understand their numbers. Stop guessing. Start pricing.

**Sections in app:**
1. **P&L Calculator (Sections 1-2)** -- Revenue, COGS, overhead. Sliders to adjust. Goal indicators show green when hitting targets (COGS <=45%, GP >=55%, OH 25-30%, Net >=20%).
2. **Capacity & SSR (Section 3)** -- Vans, hours/week, billable efficiency. Builds burden rate, adds growth markup, adds consumables = SSR.
3. **Projection (Section 4)** -- Target revenue with growth, broken into month/week/day. Marketing budget at 10%.
4. **War Chest (Section 5)** -- Profit first sweep, 60-90 day runway goal.
5. **Price Pie (Section 6)** -- What every dollar breaks down into. Four slices: COGS, overhead, profit, growth.
6. **SSR Option Pricing Calc (Section 7)** -- Quick-quote a single option. SSR x hours + specialty + third-party.
7. **Gross Profit Tracker (Section 8)** -- Log completed jobs. See if actual COGS beats the target from the Price Pie.

**Win:** "You know your number cold and price with zero hesitation."
**GPT:** Simple Service Rate GPT (PriceBot)

---

### Week 2: BrandStand

**Purpose:** Take control of lead flow. Community, referrals, past homeowners. No praying the phone rings.

**Sections in app:**
1. **Why Marketing? (Section 1)** -- The Scale Rate Graph. Resistance vs value. The Runway Principle: $300K runs on luck, $1M needs systems, $3M needs a brand, $10M needs market trust.
2. **Three Lead Engines (Section 2)** -- Organic, Word of Mouth, Paid. Traffic light status dots.
3. **Build My Runway (Section 3)** -- Nesta audit link, Smart Leads GPT link, STS Social Trust System.
4. **Funnel Numbers (Section 4)** -- Revenue target, avg ticket, close rate, booking rate, answering rate, CPL. Auto-calculates leads needed. "75% of callers hire whoever answers first."
5. **Lead Source Allocation (Section 6)** -- Where leads come from now vs needed. Gap card shows cost to fill with ads.
6. **Lead Source Tracker (Section 7)** -- Log every lead. KPIs, pie chart, trend line, pace vs goal.

**Win:** "Your calendar starts filling without touching Meta or Google."
**GPT:** Smart Leads GPT

---

### Week 3: Service Loop (Offer Loop)

**Purpose:** Build offers homeowners actually want to buy. Options, memberships, financing, serviceability.

**Sections in app:**
1. **The Service Loop (Section 1)** -- The double entendre: extra wire for future serviceability = extra service. The 2x3x.5 rule: Twice the price, three times the value, sell to half = 3x revenue.
2. **The Offer Math (Section 2)** -- Three concentric rings: Avg Offer leads -> Avg Opportunity follows -> Avg Ticket follows.
3. **Value-Squared Square (Section 3)** -- Four quadrants. MAKE: Clearer Picture + Trusted Advisor. MIND: Time Bomb + Workhorse.
4. **Financial Side (Section 4)** -- Price (SSR), Deposits (50% up front), Financing (win-win-win, good debt).
5. **Value Side (Section 5)** -- Guarantees (satisfaction + lifetime craftsmanship), Membership (1.5x SSR, front-of-line, extended hours, annual inspection), Menu (six options).
6. **The Menu (Section 6)** -- Six tiers: Platinum/Gold (premium), Silver/Bronze (mid-range), Economy/Basic. Build from bottom up. Present top-down.
7. **STYO (Section 7)** -- Lock in commitments: SSR, deposit %, financing, membership, guarantees, options count.

**Win:** "Your first multi-option presentation."
**GPTs:** Simple Options GPT, Simple Service Rate GPT

---

### Week 4: Close Loop (Sales Flow)

**Purpose:** Learn the sales flow. The 4 Squares. Serve first, sales follow.

**Sections in app:**
1. **The 4 Square Process (Section 1)** -- Equalize -> Understand -> Offer -> Survey. Must-asks and must-says per square. "Objections are eliminated at the table -- before the menu ever opens."
2. **The 4 Forces (Section 2)** -- Want (past positive), Wish (future positive), Pain (past negative), Fear (future negative). The quadrant.
3. **4-Squared Offer Setup (Section 3)** -- 4 What's x 4 Forces = 16 points of connection.
4. **Objection Handling (Section 4)** -- Acknowledge -> Align -> Pivot -> Ask. Decode reactions vs objections.
5. **Objection Decoder (Section 5)** -- 9 toggle objections with full handles.
6. **Sales Goals (Section 5)** -- Sliders for revenue, ticket, close rate, vans, calls/day. Three KPI tiers.
7. **Sales Tracker (Section 6)** -- Log every sale with client attribution back to lead source.

**Win:** "You're making friends with a simple flow, serving at a higher level, and getting sales."
**GPT:** Simple Sales GPT (role-play practice)

### Week 6: Game Plan / Graduation

**Purpose:** Synthesis. Direction. Your next rung. Map the next 12 months to your numbers.

**Sections in app:**
1. **Point A -> Point B (Section 1)** -- The hill visual. A is where you are, B is where you're going, C's are wins/insights along the way.
2. **Above the Line (Section 2)** -- B.E.D. (Blame, Excuses, Denial) vs O.A.R. (Ownership, Accountability, Responsibility).
3. **Breaking, Coasting, or Accelerating (Section 3)** -- What each choice costs. "Will I regret this later?"
4. **Your Pace (Section 4)** -- Past: Luggage -> Backseat -> Passenger -> Driver. Future: Brake -> Coast -> Accelerate -> Full Send.
5. **$1M Master Plan (Section 5)** -- Three systems (Leads/Sales/Installs), three pillars each (9 total), traffic light scoring. Score out of 27.
   - Leads: Organic, Word of Mouth, Paid
   - Sales: Conductor (YES Conductor, 4 Forces), Closure (4 Squares, objection handling), Connector (trust, empathy, rapport)
   - Installs: Work Order Process, Quality Assurance, Survey (reviews, referrals, loop close)
6. **Your Game Plan (Section 6)** -- Lightning bolt stages (Spark -> Pro -> Master -> Elite). Point A with revenue, team size, "why at A." Point B with "what needs to unlock."
7. **Top 3 Priorities (Section 7)** -- Area, problem, outcome, 3 actions. Inner Loop class recommendations auto-populate.
8. **Six Weeks of Superpowers (Section 8)** -- Select priority + action for each week.

**Win:** "You walk out with the next 12 months mapped -- and a clear picture of the operator you're becoming."
**The close:** "This isn't the finish line. It's the beginning. Service DNA isn't something you learn once. It's something you practise until it becomes who you are."

## 15. CORE TEACHING FRAMEWORKS

### The Loop Method (SLE's core IP)
- **4 What's:** What they called for, What's directly connected, What's working, What else
- **4 Forces:** Want (past+), Wish (future+), Pain (past-), Fear (future-)
- **4 Squares:** Equalize -> Understand -> Offer -> Survey
- **6-Option Model:** Platinum/Gold/Silver/Bronze/Economy/Basic (3 buyer archetypes x 2 options each)
- **Deal Formula:** 4 What's x 4 Forces = 16 points of connection
- **2x3x.5 Rule:** Twice the price, three times the value, sell to half = 3x revenue

### Key Teaching Lines
- "Objections are eliminated at the table -- before the menu ever opens."
- "I don't expect you to take this top option, but I wanted to offer the best."
- "No one likes to be sold, but everyone likes to buy." -- Joseph
- "One choice is an ultimatum. One choice is how you sleep on the couch."
- "I'd rather be told no today than lie awake at night wondering what happened."
- "Walk. Observe. Photograph. Ask questions. Build silently. Present once."

### The 4-Whats Observation Engine (feeds the options)
1. What they called you for -- restate to confirm
2. What's directly connected -- wire, breaker, panel feeding the ask
3. What's working -- the good news (levels out the emotional plane)
4. What else -- everything outside connected scope

### Emotional Nosedive (Joseph's airplane metaphor)
If you only give bad news, the customer's emotion tanks. "Level out the plane" with What's Working before surfacing What Else. The fix: "Everything else in the home looks great... this just looks like the last circuit they might have left in the house."
