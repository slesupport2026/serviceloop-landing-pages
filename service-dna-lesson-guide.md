## Week 1: Profit First

**Purpose:** Build their Simple Service Rate and understand their numbers. Stop guessing. Start pricing.

**Sections to walk through:**

1. **P&L Calculator (Sections 1-2)** — Revenue, COGS, overhead. Sliders to adjust. Goal indicators show green when hitting targets (COGS ≤45%, GP ≥55%, OH 25-30%, Net ≥20%).
   - *Teaching:* Have them enter their real numbers. Most will be shocked at their actual net margin.

2. **Capacity & SSR (Section 3)** — Vans, hours/week, billable efficiency. Builds the burden rate, adds growth markup, adds consumables = SSR.
   - *Teaching:* The SSR is the number they'll use from now on. Price in front of the customer, never in the van. Never use round numbers.

3. **Projection (Section 4)** — Target revenue with growth, broken into month/week/day. Marketing budget at 10%.
   - *Teaching:* "Here it is, sliced down so it's not scary."

4. **War Chest (Section 5)** — Profit first sweep, 60-90 day runway goal.
   - *Teaching:* Growth money gets parked, not spent. Separate account. Nest egg before any big investment.

5. **Price Pie (Section 6)** — What every dollar of their price breaks down into. Four slices: COGS, overhead, profit, growth. Target revenue shown at top with dollar amounts.
   - *Teaching:* "Profit and growth are a risk profile before they're ever take-home. The green and navy slices are your safety nets."

6. **SSR Option Pricing Calc (Section 7)** — Quick-quote a single option. SSR × hours + specialty + third-party.
   - *Teaching:* Practice pricing a real job together in class. Live, at the table.

7. **Gross Profit Tracker (Section 8)** — Log completed jobs. See if actual COGS beats the target from the Price Pie.
   - *Teaching:* "Job's done — what did it really make?" Track every job. The GP% tells you if you're pricing right or bleeding.

**Win for the week:** "You know your number cold and price with zero hesitation."

**GPT to reference:** Simple Service Rate GPT (PriceBot)

## Week 2: BrandStand™

**Purpose:** Take control of lead flow. Community, referrals, past homeowners. No praying the phone rings.

**Sections to walk through:**

1. **Why Marketing? (Section 1)** — The Scale Rate Graph. Resistance starts high, value builds over time. Where value exceeds resistance = the sale happens.
   - *Teaching:* "The marketplace has inherent resistance built in. If it didn't, we'd all be broke." Your runway melts that resistance.
   - The three reasons they called: first person they call (organic + paid), only person they call (WOM), the reason they called (STS content).
   - The Runway Principle: $300K runs on luck, $1M needs systems, $3M needs a brand, $10M needs market trust.

2. **Three Lead Engines (Section 2)** — Organic, Word of Mouth, Paid. Traffic light status dots for each channel.
   - *Teaching:* Walk through each engine's channels. Have them self-rate with the dots. "Be honest — where are you?"

3. **Build My Runway (Section 3)** — Nesta audit link, Smart Leads GPT link.
   - *Teaching:* Step 1 = audit your digital storefront. Step 2 = start your STS social trust system. Five posts today.

4. **Funnel Numbers (Section 4)** — Revenue target, avg ticket, close rate, booking rate, answering rate, CPL. Auto-calculates leads needed.
   - *Teaching:* "75% of callers hire whoever answers first." Watch the missed-call warning.

5. **Lead Source Allocation (Section 6)** — Where leads come from now vs needed. The gap card shows what it costs to fill with ads.
   - *Teaching:* Runway Principle in action — if organic/WOM is mostly red, extending the runway comes before pouring fuel.

6. **Lead Source Tracker (Section 7)** — Log every lead. KPIs, pie chart, trend line, pace vs goal.
   - *Teaching:* "It's a scoreboard, not a CRM." Log every lead that enters the business.

**Win for the week:** "Your calendar starts filling without touching Meta or Google."

**GPT to reference:** Smart Leads GPT

## Week 3: Service Loop™

**Purpose:** Build offers homeowners actually want to buy. Options, memberships, financing, serviceability.

**Sections to walk through:**

1. **The Service Loop (Section 1)** — The double entendre: extra wire for future serviceability = extra service for the future. The 2×3×.5 rule. Financial side (price, deposits, financing) and value side (guarantees, menu, membership).
   - *Teaching:* "A service loop is that little bit extra for future serviceability. That's the message of our service too."

2. **The Offer Math (Section 2)** — Three concentric rings. Avg Offer leads → Avg Opportunity follows → Avg Ticket follows. "If you offer more, on average they will spend more on average."
   - *Teaching:* Avg Offer is the ring that leads. Track all three. They grow together.

3. **Value² Square (Section 3)** — Four quadrants. MAKE: Clearer Picture + Trusted Advisor. MIND: Time Bomb + Workhorse.
   - *Teaching:* "Two things you make (add value) and two things you mind (stop draining it)."

4. **Financial Side (Section 4)** — Price (from Profit First), Deposits (50% up front), Financing (win-win-win, good debt).
   - *Teaching:* "The deposit covers labor and materials before you start. Cash flow solved."

5. **Value Side (Section 5)** — Guarantees (satisfaction + lifetime craftsmanship), Membership (1.5× SSR, front-of-line, extended hours, annual inspection), Menu (six options brief).
   - *Teaching:* "Selling Platinum without a membership is a dead end."

6. **The Menu (Section 6)** — Six tiers: Platinum/Gold (premium), Silver/Bronze (mid-range), Economy/Basic. Build from bottom up.
   - *Teaching:* "One choice is an ultimatum. One choice is how you sleep on the couch." Price from bottom up. Odd numbers. In front of the customer.

7. **STYO (Section 7)** — Lock in commitments: SSR, deposit %, financing, membership, guarantees, options count.
   - *Teaching:* "What will you commit to right now? Stick to your offer."

**Win for the week:** "Your first multi-option presentation."

**GPTs to reference:** Simple Options GPT, Simple Service Rate GPT

## Week 4: Close Loop

**Purpose:** Learn the sales flow. The 4 Squares. Serve first, sales follow.

**Sections to walk through:**

1. **The 4 Square Process (Section 1)** — Equalize → Understand → Offer → Survey. Must-asks and must-says on each card.
   - *Teaching:* "Objections are eliminated at the table — before the menu ever opens." Walk through each square's questions live.
   - Square 1: Be a student, not an expert. Guard drops when you ask, not tell.
   - Square 2: The why tells you what to offer. Without it you're guessing.
   - Square 3: Build the menu, present the options, handle objections HERE. Ask three times.
   - Square 4: "You'll feel the pull to leave. This is what you've earned. Stay longer."

2. **The 4 Forces (Section 2)** — Want (past positive), Wish (future positive), Pain (past negative), Fear (future negative). The quadrant.
   - *Teaching:* "Mirror their language. When you use their words, they feel understood."

3. **4² Offer Setup (Section 3)** — 4 What's × 4 Forces = 16 points of connection. Lace the forces into each "what."
   - *Teaching:* Each "what" gets a force and an opening line. Practice these out loud.

4. **Objection Handling (Section 4)** — The line metaphor. Acknowledge → Align → Pivot → Ask. Decode reactions vs objections.
   - *Teaching:* "If you go on the defensive, everything gets worse. Get to their side of the line first."

5. **Objection Decoder (Section 5)** — 9 toggle objections with full handles.
   - *Teaching:* Pick 2-3 and role-play in pairs. Rehearse out loud before the next call.

6. **Sales Goals (Section 5)** — Sliders for revenue, ticket, close rate, vans, calls/day. Three KPI tiers.
   - *Teaching:* "Same concentric rings from the offer math — but now measured."

7. **Sales Tracker (Section 6)** — Log every sale with client attribution back to lead source.
   - *Teaching:* "Which lead sources actually close? This is how you find out."

**Win for the week:** "You're making friends with a simple flow, serving at a higher level, and getting sales."

**GPTs to reference:** Simple Sales GPT (role-play practice)

## Week 6: Game Plan

**Purpose:** Synthesis. Direction. Your next rung. Map the next 12 months to your numbers.

**Sections to walk through:**

1. **Point A → Point B (Section 1)** — The hill visual. A is where you are, B is where you're going, C's are the wins and insights along the way.
   - *Teaching:* "Every person you'll ever meet has the same challenge. They're at A. They want to reach B."

2. **Above the Line (Section 2)** — B.E.D. (Blame, Excuses, Denial) vs O.A.R. (Ownership, Accountability, Responsibility).
   - *Teaching:* "Name the line. Three letters, no shame, instant reset."

3. **Breaking, Coasting, or Accelerating (Section 3)** — What each choice costs.
   - *Teaching:* The cost of quitting is another open cycle. "Will I regret this later?"

4. **Your Pace (Section 4)** — Past pace (Luggage → Backseat → Passenger → Driver). Future pace (Brake → Coast → Accelerate → Full Send).
   - *Teaching:* "No shame. Just truth. Now — what do you choose from here?"

5. **$1M Master Plan (Section 5)** — Three systems (Leads/Sales/Installs), three pillars each, traffic light scoring. Score out of 27.
   - *Teaching:* "Before you rate — do I have a complete, written system that we actually follow?"

6. **Your Game Plan (Section 6)** — Lightning bolt stages (Spark → Pro → Master → Elite). Point A with revenue, team size, "why at A." Point B with "what needs to unlock."
   - *Teaching:* Walk through the stages. "Where are you? Where are you driving? Why?"

7. **Top 3 Priorities (Section 7)** — Area, problem, outcome, 3 actions. Inner Loop class recommendations auto-populate.
   - *Teaching:* "What problem are we solving? What does done look like?" No "trying" — actions.

8. **Six Weeks of Superpowers (Section 8)** — Select priority + action for each week.
   - *Teaching:* "This is your playbook for the next six weeks. Map it."

**Win for the week:** "You walk out with the next 12 months mapped — and a clear picture of the operator you're becoming."

**The close:** "This isn't the finish line. It's the beginning. Service DNA isn't something you learn once. It's something you practise until it becomes who you are."
